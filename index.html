425°FOven
~25 minChicken + vegetables
165°FChicken inside

Ingredients

Instructions

1.

Roast: Toss chicken and vegetables separately with their listed oil, seasoning, and salt. Roast on separate trays. Check chicken reaches 165°F.

2.

Grains: Cook rice and bulgur separately, following package directions.

3.

Stovetop: Heat spinach and drain excess water. Simmer beans with tomatoes (including liquid), oil, seasoning, and salt.

4.

Portion: Divide evenly into 1 container.

Refrigerate up to 3–4 days or freeze. Reheat to 165°F.
