Uses 1 tsp total seasoning blend per batch.

How to cook it

  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.

    Set out separate trays for the chicken and vegetables.

  2. 2
    Roast the chicken.

    Coat the thighs with their allocated olive oil, seasoning blend, and salt. Roast about 20–30 minutes, until the thickest part reaches 165°F.

  3. 3
    Roast the vegetables.

    Toss broccoli and chopped carrots with their allocated oil, seasoning, and salt. Roast at 425°F for 20–25 minutes.

  4. 4
    Cook both grains separately.

    Prepare the dry basmati rice and dry bulgur according to their respective package directions.

  5. 5
    Finish the stovetop items.

    Heat spinach in a skillet and remove excess water. In another pan, simmer drained black beans with diced tomatoes (including tomato liquid), their allocated oil and seasoning, and salt.

  6. 6
    Divide into containers.

    Portion all components evenly across 1 meal-prep container.

Storage & safety

Refrigerate for up to 3–4 days or freeze promptly for longer storage. Reheat to 165°F. Bigger batches may require additional oven trays or rounds; the safe internal temperature matters more than the clock.

Egg & oat scramble, yogurt & fruit

One full breakfast, with the scramble hot and the yogurt bowl cold.

Quick reference: microwave the eggs, oats, and milk together. Serve the yogurt, berries, and chia seeds separately.

Ingredients · 1 breakfast

1

Egg & oat scramble

  • Large eggs3
  • Dry rolled oats50 g
  • Whole milk60 mL
  • Salt and pepperTo taste
  • Hot sauceOptional
2

Greek yogurt & fruit

  • Plain nonfat Greek yogurt150 g
  • Frozen mixed berries Thawed100 g
  • Chia seeds10 g

How to make it

  1. 1
    Mix the scramble.

    Combine the eggs, dry oats, and whole milk in a microwave-safe bowl. Add salt and pepper.

  2. 2
    Microwave until cooked.

    Cook in short intervals, stirring between them, until the eggs are fully set. Add hot sauce if desired.

  3. 3
    Assemble the cold side.

    Put the yogurt, thawed berries, and chia seeds together. Eat alongside the scramble.

Microwave times vary by appliance and bowl size, so this plan uses doneness rather than a fixed time.
